To My Beautiful Girl ❤️

Dearest Princess,
My Love, I don't know where to begin, so let me begin at the start — 11th grade, computer class. I remember noticing you before I really knew you, thinking to myself that you were cute, having no idea what was coming. We stayed just casual friends for the rest of that year and into the next, but I noticed you. Every single day, I noticed you. Then in 12th, you started sitting in front of me. I used to just look at your hair and think about how much I liked it — such a small thing, but it stayed with me. And then one day, out of nowhere, you asked me for chaar chitthi. I said yes without thinking twice, and I had no idea that one small moment would change everything. From that day on, something shifted in me. I started thinking about you more and more, until I couldn't stop. I began sitting behind you even at lunch, not talking, not doing anything — just quietly taking in how beautiful you were. Then came Traditional Day. I saw you in that saree and I was completely stunned. I don't think I've ever forgotten that feeling. And then the day with Hetvi and that fight happened, and somehow that became the turning point for us. After that, you — who were still just my friend then — and I started talking more and more, six, seven hours a day, like it was the easiest thing in the world. I still think about you waiting for me outside Aakash's for pani puri, and how I made you wait fifteen, twenty minutes because my maths sir wouldn't let me go. I still feel a little guilty about that. And then came 6th January 2025, 1:50 in the afternoon. You told me how you felt, and I acted like I didn't understand — I don't even know why I did that. But that same day, we both confessed to each other, and we talked for almost ten hours straight, neither of us wanting to hang up. Going to the mela with you on 23rd January was one of the happiest, most overwhelming days I have ever lived. I still don't have the words for how full my heart felt that day. And two days later, on my birthday, you gave me that Naruto keychain. I have never lost it, and I never will. Out of everything anyone has ever given me, it is the most precious thing I own — not because of what it is, but because of who gave it to me. So on this Girlfriend's Day, I just want to say it plainly: I love you. I care about you more than I know how to put into words. All I want, every single day, is for you to be happy. I am not going anywhere — not now, not ever, no matter what life throws at us. I will keep listening to you, laughing with you, and cherishing every ordinary day we get together, because you have made every one of them extraordinary. And yes, I'm still going to irritate you sometimes. I can't help it — you're simply too cute when you're angry at me. Happy Girlfriend's Day, my love. ❤️
Forever Yours,
❤️ Your Prince
